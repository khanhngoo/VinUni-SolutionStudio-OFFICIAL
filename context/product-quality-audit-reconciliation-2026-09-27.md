# Product-quality audit reconciliation — 27 September 2026

## Scope and method

This document reconciles [the 10 September pre-demo audit](pre-demo-product-quality-audit.md) with [the 27 September browser audit](browser-product-quality-audit-2026-09-27.md) against the current repository. It covers **every P0/P1 finding** in the older audit, including its compact concurrency and seed-data findings, and **F1–F9** in the latest audit. `AGENTS.md`, the roadmap, seed manifest, and relevant routes, components, services, mutations, and schema modules were reviewed. No application code, schema, migration, seed, or database data was changed for this reconciliation.

Targeted read-only Playwright MCP checks on the running local app confirmed: anonymous `/challenges` shows one PUBLIC_PREVIEW challenge; Jordan sees eight, including a Closed card and the synthetic E-Lab card; Jordan's route offer is lapsed; faculty and partner identities land on `/challenges` after sign-in; a faculty user opening an offer and Jordan opening a malformed assessment ID each receive HTTP 500; Bến Cảng's default `/partner/students` returns 404 while `?challenge=route-optimisation` loads. The partner deck still says “AI shortlist.” All other classifications below rest on current code/fixture inspection and the cited earlier browser evidence; they are not claimed as fresh browser reproductions. Destructive transitions were not exercised against the shared database.

Classification key: **RESOLVED** means the finding is fixed end-to-end; **STILL OPEN** means its relevant code path persists; **PARTIALLY RESOLVED** means some user-facing capability now exists but the finding remains; **SUPERSEDED** means the old diagnosis/recommendation is no longer the right requirement; **REQUIRES PRODUCT DECISION** means the observed gap persists but its intended behavior is undecided. None of the requested findings is fully RESOLVED in this checkout.

The older audit's suggested “fix” of making four or five challenges public and broadening external-partner marketplace access conflicts with Phase 6.3. The [current policy](../src/services/challenge-policy.ts) intentionally gives anonymous and external-partner ordinary browsing **PUBLIC_PREVIEW only**. Preserve that boundary. A one-card public catalog can still be a UX concern, but it is not evidence of an authorization defect.

## Decisions required before implementation

1. Is self-service registration intended to lead to a verified student identity, or only a basic/partner account? Do not grant student access merely because someone signs up.
2. Is one PUBLIC_PREVIEW challenge the intended anonymous catalog? If not, choose *which* additional briefs are safe to publish; do not broaden VINUNI_ONLY/private visibility by default.
3. May a partner source candidates for its **own** PRIVATE challenge? If yes, that requires an owner-scoped read path separate from ordinary marketplace access.
4. Are faculty supervision requests hard-expired at `respondBy`, or may a late response be recorded? UI and server must agree.
5. Who grades assessments, what is the authoritative passing threshold, and is the assessment stage in the stakeholder demo? A threshold/schema change requires DBML review before migration.
6. Is challenge rejection terminal, and what recovery/repost path should the partner see? Are multiple milestone review events legitimate history after revision?
7. Must the demo show genuine signup-to-project writes, or a clearly scoped seeded tour? This controls the priority of selection, grading, and project-creation work.
8. What is the DEMO date convention: fixed, durable future business dates or a moving seed clock? Runtime must continue using the real server clock; do not repair drift by pinning production `TODAY`.
9. Which intentionally ineligible/unknown-GPA/empty-pipeline scenarios should remain as negative or historical examples? Do not invent academic data merely to fill the UI.

The older audit also praised a global admin console, but `src/app/admin` is absent here. The untracked [admin console plan](admin-console-implementation-plan.md) is not an implementation; do not assume a platform-owner grading or management route exists.
